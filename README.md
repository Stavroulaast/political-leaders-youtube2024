## Political Leaders on YouTube: Content and engagement analysis
### Course Project: Digital Tools in New Media | MA Digital Media, Culture and Communication, Aristotle University of Thessaloniki |  Fall Semester 2023-2024

This project examines how elected political leaders in Greece use YouTube as a platform for political communication and online engagement. Using the YouTube API, Python programming, and AI-assisted text analysis (ChatGPT), the study explores what type of content parliamentary leaders uploaded during 2023 and how digital tools can support new media research.

*Data were collected on 31 January 2024 from nine party and leader channels in the Hellenic Parliament.*

### Notebooks & Files

`YoutubeAPIDataCollection.ipynb`  Data collection through the YouTube API.\
`ytDCproject-DA.ipynb`  Data cleaning, processing, analysis, and visualizations.\
`PoliticalLeadersonYoutube.pdf`  Short report summarizing results and key findings.\
`stopwords-el.json` / `stopwords-el.txt`  Greek stopwords used for AI-assisted text analysis.

### Data Files (`/data` folder)
`channelData.csv`  Channel-level information (subscribers, total videos, activity).\
`videos_data.csv`  Metadata for all uploaded videos (titles, views, publish dates).\
`elected_data.csv`  Subset of videos uploaded specifically during 2023.\
`uploads_per_month.csv`  Monthly upload frequency from channel launch until January 2024.
